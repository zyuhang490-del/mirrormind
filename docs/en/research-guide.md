## Research background

Two Chinese documents provide the detailed background and reading plan:

- [Research background and intellectual development](../../Mirrormind设计文档/视野拓展与专业积淀/视野拓展与专业积淀.md).
- [Literature reading roadmap](../../Mirrormind设计文档/视野拓展与专业积淀/阅读路线图.md).

The background essay groups material into philosophy, scientific foundations, related projects, and technical directions. Its proposed connections to MirrorMind are interpretations to examine critically, not proof that a philosopher or scientific theory endorses the project.

| Area in the source | Examples discussed | Review question for MirrorMind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alienation and technology | Marx, Marcuse, Heidegger | Could a system intended to support people instead turn them into resources or reinforce conformity? |
| Memory and technological mediation | Stiegler, Bergson, McLuhan | How does external memory change action, and what role should forgetting retain? |
| Difference, individuation, and situated perspectives | Deleuze, Simondon, Haraway | How can the system support change without assuming a finished, fixed self? |
| Self-practice | Foucault | How might a tool shape the person using it, rather than simply serve existing preferences? |
| Mind, uncertainty, and embodiment | Friston, Clark, Chalmers, Varela, Edelman, Hofstadter | What would count as evidence for a cognitive hypothesis, and where does a software analogy stop? |
| Systems and information | Wiener, Shannon, Holland; Bateson in the roadmap | How should feedback, communication, and emergence inform a concrete design? |
| Historical human–computer interaction | Bush and Licklider | How can associative memory and complementary collaboration inform an interaction prototype? |
| Related projects and research communities | MyLifeBits, historical discussion of Rewind AI and Recall, linked-note tools, Quantified Self, CHI/UIST/DIS | Which functions are precedented, and what user need still requires validation? |
| Candidate techniques | LoRA/PEFT, DSPy, contextual bandits | Does a demonstrated prototype need these techniques, and what evidence would justify adding them? |

These names reproduce topics in the archive; they are not current product recommendations or a surveyed account of the latest research.

The background includes a critical reading of The Extended Mind that questions whether MirrorMind meets the paper's conditions for treating external memory as cognitive extension. That discussion is a challenge to the project's analogy, not simple confirmation of it. Later state-representation and permission revisions should be read alongside earlier passive-recording proposals.

## The source reading sequence

The detailed Chinese roadmap proposes seven stages:

1. **Intellectual positioning:** Haraway, Bush, Licklider, Clark and Chalmers.
2. **Critical perspective:** Heidegger and Marcuse.
3. **Cognitive science:** Clark and selected Edelman chapters.
4. **Philosophical depth:** Bergson, Simondon, and Stiegler.
5. **Contemporary comparisons:** personal-memory systems, MyLifeBits, linked-note tools, and Quantified Self.
6. **Systems and mathematical foundations:** Shannon, Wiener, Bateson, and complex adaptive systems.
7. **Reflection and recalibration:** McLuhan, Foucault, Marx, Varela, Harari, and Deleuze.

Dates, durations, and checkboxes in that roadmap are historical planning records, not a fresh claim that those readings are complete. The suggested note format asks for a one-sentence account, a concept mapping, a challenge to the project, and a response from the author's perspective.

For a team reviewing the project, the core [design](initial-manifesto.md), [mechanism](self-structuring.md), [assessment](architecture-assessment.md), and [working prototype](mirrorreader.md) provide the direct entry points. The full reading program is optional background.
