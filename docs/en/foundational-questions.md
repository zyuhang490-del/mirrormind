## Organization

Organize material around stable foundational questions rather than creating a folder for every conversation. Add discussion to an existing question, or create a numbered question only when it identifies a new, sustained underlying tension.

- Engineering mechanisms belong in the design documents.
- Philosophical premises, epistemological difficulties, and contradictions engineering alone cannot resolve belong here.
- Integrate important reasoning into the relevant question rather than accumulating separate conversation transcripts.
- Update the existing file when a question develops, retaining dates and the evolution of positions.

## Question 1: consciousness, information exchange, and material organization

**Central question:** does AI consciousness depend on the organization of information, on a particular material carrier, or does the cyborg perspective require reframing the question itself?

Argument path:

~~~text
Cyborg reframing
    → machines participate in the ontological constitution of the acting subject
    → reopen the boundaries of consciousness
    → being-for-itself, contradiction, and self-production
    → how MirrorMind can keep contradictions visible
~~~

Current weaknesses identified in the Chinese index:

1. Participation in practice does not by itself establish that a machine has consciousness; the argument risks making that leap.
2. Dissolving boundaries remains in tension with conditions of self-reference.
3. Some claims are difficult to test.
4. Counterexamples capable of challenging the framework are still needed.

The underlying Chinese document is available as a Markdown transcription of the locally retained Word original. It is not translated into English in this edition; this section translates the source index's synopsis.

## Question 2: acting with incomplete symbolic representations

**Central question:** if a person's state cannot be fully represented symbolically, how can MirrorMind act and learn from state data without mistaking a provisional model for the person's real essence?

~~~text
Actual human state
    → data records and symbolic representation
    → irreducible descriptive remainder
    → anomalies expose model failure
    → reverse state identification
    → reversible exploratory structures
    → user correction and model revision
~~~

Provisional position:

1. Separate actual human state, raw data, interpretation, and executable structure.
2. Treat state models as temporary, local, sourced, fallible practical hypotheses.
3. Anomalies justify reopening interpretation; they do not directly prove an essence.
4. Retain descriptive remainder instead of treating it all as removable noise.
5. Let users inspect, revise, delete, and exceed representations of themselves.
6. Let uncertainty affect execution permissions and confirmation requirements.

See the [full question](symbolic-representation.md) and its [engineering counterpart](self-structuring.md).
